Agent-Vocabulary

Machine-loadable list of words an AGenNext agent can understand.

Purpose

Agent-Vocabulary stores vocabulary as Schema.org DefinedTermSet and DefinedTerm seed data.

Separation

Agent-Vocabulary = words / terms
Agent-Graph      = relationships between words
Agent-Grammar    = validation rules
Prompt-Library   = accepted CreativeWork artifacts
